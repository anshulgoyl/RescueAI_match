# Profile-image cropping guide

## Goal

Create a welcoming card image that clearly shows the individual dog, without changing the photograph or hiding important context.

## Select before cropping

Choose one approved, original image for each dog. Prefer:

- one individual dog, rather than a group or collage;
- face and eyes visible, front-facing or gentle three-quarter view;
- sharp, naturally lit image;
- enough room around the head for a tall card crop.

Avoid text overlays, collages, distant dogs, severe blur, and images where another animal or person is the visual subject.

## Crop for the match card

1. Keep the face, eyes, and ears inside the crop whenever the original image permits.
2. Start from a centered crop; move the focal point up or down only enough to protect the face.
3. Do not zoom so far that the dog becomes a partial face, unless that is the only approved source and the adoption team approves it.
4. Check the crop on desktop and mobile at the card’s tall aspect ratio.
5. Save the focal point as `cardFocus` (for example `center 23%`) with the dog profile data.

## Verify

Open the match card, profile dialog, and shortlist thumbnail. Confirm the dog is recognizable in all three. A good crop must never cut off the eyes, leave the dog unrecognizable, or imply a different dog than the official profile.

## Editorial boundary

Cropping may improve framing only. Do not use AI to add, remove, alter, or invent elements in an adoption photograph.
